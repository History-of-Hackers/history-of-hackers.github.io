Victor S. Miller

Miller’s 1985 elliptic-curve proposal became part of the technical basis of ECC, a widely deployed family of public-key systems.

Victor S. Miller is an American mathematician and cryptographer. His 1985 work proposed elliptic curves for public-key cryptography, independently of Neal Koblitz.

Cryptographic legacy

Elliptic-curve cryptography became an important approach to public-key design. Miller’s research also gave rise to the pairing computation commonly known as Miller’s algorithm.

Timeline

  • 1947Born in the United States.
  • 1975Completes a PhD in mathematics at Harvard University.
  • 1985Publishes “Use of elliptic curves in cryptography.”
  • 2004Publishes work on efficient calculation of the Weil pairing.
  • 2013Named an IACR Fellow.
  • 2020Receives the Levchin Prize in Real World Cryptography.
