Clifford Stoll

Stoll's account made long-form incident investigation understandable to a broad public and remains a key document in the history of computer espionage.

Clifford Stoll is an astronomer, author, and former systems administrator. While working at Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory in 1986, he investigated an unexplained 75-cent accounting discrepancy and followed evidence of unauthorized access.

The Cuckoo's Egg

His 1989 book The Cuckoo's Egg recounts that investigation and its connection to computer espionage. The book is an enduring primary narrative of the operational work behind an intrusion investigation.
