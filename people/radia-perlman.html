Radia Perlman

Perlman’s work made large bridged networks practical and resilient. Her writing and protocol research also helped make network-security concepts accessible to engineers.

Radia Perlman is a computer scientist and network engineer whose work on the spanning-tree algorithm and protocol design had lasting influence on Ethernet bridging. She has also written widely used material on networking and network security.

Networks and security

Reliable connectivity is a security concern as well as an operational one: protocols must retain useful behavior under failure and avoid creating dangerous loops or fragile assumptions. Perlman’s work belongs to that foundational systems layer, alongside her contributions to security education and protocol design.

Documented influence

Historical and professional organizations have recognized Perlman’s role in networking; credit for deployed standards and their evolution belongs to the broader engineering community as well.
