Hugo Krawczyk

Krawczyk's research addresses a key systems-security question: how cryptographic keys are derived, authenticated, and exchanged without invalidating the guarantees of the algorithms around them.

Hugo Krawczyk is a cryptographer associated with IBM Research. His work includes key-derivation functions, authentication, secure key exchange, and formal analysis of practical protocols.

Protocol building blocks

Krawczyk authored the HKDF specification, a widely cited extract-and-expand approach to deriving cryptographic keys. Key derivation is often a quiet but crucial layer between raw shared secrets and protocol keys.

Security significance

His work helps connect security proofs to protocol components used in deployed network systems, including TLS-related key-exchange research.

Timeline

  • 1997Co-authors work on HMAC and related constructions.
  • 2010Publishes RFC 5869, HMAC-based Extract-and-Expand Key Derivation Function (HKDF).
  • 2015Receives RSA Conference's Award for Excellence in Mathematics.
  • 2017Named an IBM Fellow.
