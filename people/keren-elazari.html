Keren Elazari

Elazari has helped bring a research-informed hacker perspective to public audiences, connecting disclosure, technical curiosity and policy without treating “hacker” as a single or uniformly benign category.

Keren Elazari is an Israeli cybersecurity researcher and speaker associated with research on cyber conflict, hacker culture and vulnerability research. Her public work argues that independent researchers can expose weaknesses that organizations must address.

Research and communication

Elazari’s work is significant as translation between technical security communities and wider policy audiences. It should not be read as a blanket endorsement of unauthorized access: responsible research depends on scope, disclosure practices and harm reduction.

Academic affiliation

Her public profiles identify her with Tel Aviv University’s interdisciplinary cyber-research community and with work as an analyst and educator.
