Nigel Smart

Practical security protocols need both mathematical rigor and deployable engineering. Smart's work examines how public-key and multi-party techniques can move from theoretical constructions into real systems.

Nigel Smart is a full professor at KU Leuven's COSIC, the university's Computer Security and Industrial Cryptography group.

From theory to practice

Smart describes his research as work at the interface of public-key cryptography and cryptographic protocols, with an emphasis on turning theoretical constructions into practical solutions.

Research areas

His work has included elliptic-curve and pairing-based cryptography, key agreement, post-quantum cryptography, multi-party computation, and fully homomorphic encryption.

Community work

Smart co-founded the Real World Cryptography conference series, which brings applied and theoretical cryptography communities together around practical deployment concerns.
