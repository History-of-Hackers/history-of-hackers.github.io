Ronald Cramer

Cramer's work on public-key encryption and secure computation shows how algebraic theory can yield schemes with stronger, explicitly stated protections against chosen-ciphertext attacks.

Ronald Cramer is head of the Cryptology Group at Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica (CWI) and a professor at Leiden University. His research ranges from foundational cryptology to applications of secure computation.

Public-key security

The Cramer-Shoup encryption system is a landmark construction because it provided an efficient public-key scheme with a formal chosen-ciphertext security proof under standard assumptions.

Academic leadership

His work and institutional roles have supported research into encryption, digital signatures, secure computation, and post-quantum security questions.

Timeline

  • 1997Completes a Ph.D. at the University of Amsterdam.
  • 1998Introduces the Cramer-Shoup encryption scheme with Victor Shoup.
  • 2004Becomes head of CWI's Cryptology Group and professor at Leiden University.
  • 2013Named an IACR Fellow.
