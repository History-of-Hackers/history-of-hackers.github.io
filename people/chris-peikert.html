Chris Peikert

Lattice assumptions are a major basis for cryptographic designs intended to withstand known quantum algorithms. Peikert's research has helped develop, explain, and analyze constructions based on those assumptions.

Chris Peikert is a cryptographer whose research includes lattices, coding theory, algorithms, and computational complexity. His academic materials identify lattice-based cryptographic schemes as a particular focus.

Lattices and security

Peikert's work studies cryptosystems whose security is based on the apparent difficulty of lattice problems. His publications include work on Learning With Errors and ring-based variants.

Research and teaching

He received a Ph.D. from MIT in 2006 and has taught and published in cryptography and information security. His research record includes collaborations on foundational lattice constructions.

Enduring relevance

As standards and implementations consider post-quantum migration, lattice cryptography has become an important subject for both theoretical analysis and practical engineering.
