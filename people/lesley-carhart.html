Lesley Carhart

Carhart brings incident-response experience into environments where safety and continuity matter as much as data confidentiality. Their writing, teaching, and career mentoring have also made the operational-security community more approachable.

Overview

Lesley Carhart is an incident-response and digital-forensics practitioner focused on industrial control system and operational-technology environments. Dragos identifies Carhart as a Technical Director of Incident Response, leading investigations and proactive threat hunting in industrial networks.

Before Dragos, Carhart led incident response at Motorola Solutions. Their work covers commodity, targeted, and insider-threat investigations, a scope that demonstrates the operational complexity of responding to incidents in industrial settings.

Carhart also teaches, writes, and organizes career-oriented activities at conferences. This combination of technical response work and community support has made them a significant voice in industrial cybersecurity.
