David Kernell

A federal jury convicted Kernell in 2010 of misdemeanor unauthorized access and obstruction of justice. The DOJ recorded a sentence of one year and one day.

David Kernell was a University of Tennessee student involved in the 2008 access to Sarah Palin’s Yahoo email account.

Prosecution

A federal jury convicted Kernell in April 2010 of misdemeanor unauthorized access and obstruction of justice.

The DOJ said evidence showed he learned of a possible FBI investigation after the access.

Legacy

The case received national attention during the 2008 presidential campaign and later federal proceedings.
