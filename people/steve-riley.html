Steve Riley

Riley has been an influential communicator of how security controls, risk decisions, and organizational culture interact in large technology environments.

Security strategy

Steve Riley is a security strategist and author whose public work has addressed how organizations make security decisions at scale. His approach emphasizes that security is not a product purchase alone: it involves architecture, operations, user experience, and risk governance.

Writing

Riley co-authored Security Strategy: From Requirements to Reality, a Microsoft Press book that presents security planning as a process of translating business and technical requirements into defensible controls.

Public communication

Through talks and writing, Riley has helped make enterprise-security concepts accessible to technical and non-technical audiences. His work belongs to the practitioner tradition of turning security principles into organizational habits.
