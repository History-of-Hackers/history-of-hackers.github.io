Elisa Bertino

Bertino’s work helped establish database security and access control as rigorous, broad research areas.

Elisa Bertino is a computer scientist at Purdue University and a former research director at CERIAS. Her research spans information security and database systems, including access control, secure data publishing, privacy, and distributed systems.

Protecting data systems

Bertino’s work combines theoretical and practical approaches to securing data and governing access to it. Her contributions have informed research on role-based access control, privacy, and security for data-intensive systems.
