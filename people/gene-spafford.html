Gene Spafford

Spafford combined incident analysis, defensive tools, education, and institutional leadership to help make computer security a sustained interdisciplinary academic field.

Eugene H. “Gene” Spafford is a Purdue computer-security researcher, distinguished professor, and executive director emeritus of the Center for Education and Research in Information Assurance and Security (CERIAS).

Research and defensive foundations

Spafford researches intrusion detection and response, security policy, architecture, forensics, vulnerability analysis, and education. Purdue credits his research group with early work on security scanners, intrusion-detection tools, integrity controls, and firewall models.

Institution building and writing

He founded and led a major academic security center and has authored or contributed to books including Practical UNIX and Internet Security. His career shows how security advances through research, operations, education, and public engagement together.

Timeline

  • 1987Joins Purdue University faculty.
  • 1991COAST, a predecessor to CERIAS, is established at Purdue.
  • 1999CERIAS is established, with Spafford as founding executive director.
  • 2000Receives the NIST/NCSC National Computer Systems Security Award.
