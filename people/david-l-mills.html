David L. Mills

Accurate, authenticated time is a security dependency for logging, certificates, distributed systems, and incident response. Mills made network time synchronization an enduring part of Internet infrastructure.

American computer engineer and internet pioneer who developed the Network Time Protocol (NTP) and taught computer security and networking at the University of Delaware.

Historical contribution

Accurate, authenticated time is a security dependency for logging, certificates, distributed systems, and incident response. Mills made network time synchronization an enduring part of Internet infrastructure.

In memoriam

David L. Mills died in 2024. This archive remembers their contribution with care and credits the historical record on which this account relies.
