David J. Farber

Farber helped connect technical network research with education and public policy, shaping institutions and infrastructure that made large-scale networked computing possible.

David J. Farber was an American computer scientist and professor whose career included programming languages, computer communications, research networks, and internet policy. The University of Pennsylvania credits him with work on early distributed systems and with leadership around CSNET, NSFNET, and high-speed networking initiatives.

Network-building legacy

Farber’s career illustrates how standards, research networks, and public institutions jointly formed the internet. He also served as the U.S. Federal Communications Commission’s Chief Technologist from 2000 to 2001.

In memoriam

Farber died in 2026. His work remains part of the technical and civic history of networked computing.
