Joxean Koret

Koret's career bridges database security, malware analysis, and reverse-engineering tools. His public talks and technical work illustrate how deep software analysis can surface flaws in complex, widely deployed products.

Overview

Joxean Koret is a Spanish computer-security researcher whose work has covered reverse engineering, malware analysis, and vulnerability research. Conference biographies describe a progression from database development and administration into security research.

His research applied database knowledge to finding vulnerabilities in major vendors' products, particularly Oracle software. He also worked on anti-malware development and on IDA Pro at Hex-Rays, placing him close to tools used by many software-security researchers.

Koret has shared work through conferences and technical communities. In 2020 he co-authored Mystique, presented in Black Hat Arsenal, a project focused on post-exploitation communications and exfiltration; its inclusion here records the research context, not an endorsement of misuse.
