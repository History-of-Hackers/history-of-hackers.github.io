Charles H. Bennett

Bennett's work helped show how the physical behavior of information can be used to establish security properties. Quantum key distribution became a major research path for testing the limits and future of cryptography.

Charles H. Bennett is an American physicist and information theorist whose work helped found quantum information science. At IBM Research, he contributed to reversible computing, quantum teleportation, and quantum cryptography.

Information and physical security

Quantum cryptography explores security mechanisms based on physical principles rather than only computational assumptions. Bennett's work with collaborators, including the BB84 quantum key-distribution protocol, made the field a major part of modern cryptographic research.

Research legacy

Quantum information does not replace the need for sound classical security engineering, but it expands the models under which cryptographers reason. Bennett's work is therefore a key bridge between information theory, physics, and security.
