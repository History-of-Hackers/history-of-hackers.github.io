Thomas Rid

Rid connects technical history to policy and conflict studies, giving readers a researched framework for interpreting claims about cyberwar and security.

Thomas Rid is a scholar and author who has written on cyber conflict, cybernetics, and information operations. His work joins historical research to analysis of the claims made about technology in warfare.

Cybernetics and cyber conflict

Rise of the Machines follows the intellectual and technological history of cybernetics, while Rid’s other work addresses cyberwar and information operations. Together, these books offer context for security history beyond individual incidents or tools.
