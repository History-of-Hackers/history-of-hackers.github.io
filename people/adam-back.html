Adam Back

Hashcash's proof-of-work idea became the foundation of Bitcoin mining and of anti-spam and anti-abuse rate limiting.

Adam Back is a British cryptographer and cypherpunk. He earned a PhD in computer science from the University of Exeter and was an active participant in the cypherpunks mailing list in the 1990s.

Hashcash

In 1997 Back proposed Hashcash, a scheme in which a sender proves it has spent CPU time by finding a partial hash collision. Intended to throttle email spam and denial-of-service, it was later cited by Satoshi Nakamoto's Bitcoin whitepaper as the basis of Bitcoin's proof-of-work mining.

Export-control activism

Back was among the cypherpunks who protested U.S. cryptography export rules by circulating a three-line Perl implementation of RSA, printed on T-shirts and email signatures as a "munition." In 2014 he co-founded Blockstream, where he serves as CEO.
