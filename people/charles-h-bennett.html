Charles H. Bennett

Charles H. Bennett is included for documented contributions to the technical and formal foundations of computer security and cryptography.

Charles H. Bennett is an IBM Fellow whose research spans the physics of information, reversible computation, and quantum information theory. His work connects the limits of computation with the design of secure communication systems.

Security relevance

Bennett and Gilles Brassard developed the BB84 protocol, a foundational quantum-key-distribution scheme. The work reframed key establishment as a problem whose security can depend on quantum measurement rather than only on computational hardness assumptions.

Timeline

  • 1973Joins IBM Research.
  • 1984Co-authors the BB84 quantum cryptography protocol.
  • 1993Co-authors the paper introducing quantum teleportation.
  • 2025Named co-recipient of the ACM A.M. Turing Award with Gilles Brassard.
