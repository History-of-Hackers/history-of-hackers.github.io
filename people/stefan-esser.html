Stefan Esser

Stefan Esser is included for documented contributions to security research and practice. The cited record situates that work within the development of modern security testing and public technical exchange.

German security researcher known for PHP security research and for the Hardened-PHP and Suhosin projects.

Research and practice

Stefan Esser has worked in areas including php-security, suhosin, web-security. Public conference material and professional records document this work without treating conference appearances as proof of every claimed result.

Contribution

The record associated with Stefan Esser is relevant to the history of hardened-php, suhosin. It reflects the role of public research, testing, and technical education in security practice.
