Fred Kaplan

Kaplan’s reporting-based history records how policymakers, military institutions, scientists, and intelligence agencies approached computer security and cyber conflict.

Fred Kaplan is a national-security journalist and author. In Dark Territory, he examines the development of United States cyber-security and cyber-conflict policy through the people and institutions involved.

A reported history of cyberwar

The book follows the relationship between computer security, intelligence, military planning, and government policy from the late Cold War onward. It is relevant to hacker history because it places hacking and defense within their institutional and strategic setting.

Timeline
