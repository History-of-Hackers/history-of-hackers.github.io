Andreas Pfitzmann

Pfitzmann helped articulate privacy as a technical security objective with explicit properties and engineering consequences.

Andreas Pfitzmann was a German computer scientist known for foundational work in privacy and computer security. He became a professor at Technische Universität Dresden in 1993 and contributed to the development of privacy-enhancing technologies and privacy-protection concepts.

Making privacy an engineering goal

His work addressed anonymity, unlinkability, and related protection goals in ways that helped give privacy engineering a more precise vocabulary. TU Dresden’s memorial notes the influence of this work on privacy protection goals beyond confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

In memoriam

Pfitzmann died in 2010. His scholarship and mentorship continue to influence people building systems that protect against unnecessary observation and linkability.
