Dorothy E. Denning

Denning’s research connected formal security models with deployable monitoring systems and brought computer-security questions into academic and policy debate.

Dorothy E. Denning is an American computer-security researcher and Emeritus Distinguished Professor of Defense Analysis at the Naval Postgraduate School. Her work spans cryptography, information-flow controls, intrusion detection, and cyber policy.

Research legacy

Her 1986 intrusion-detection model is a foundational reference for the field, and her book Cryptography and Data Security helped introduce modern cryptographic concepts to a broad computing audience. Her academic record also documents research and teaching on computer-network attack and defense.
