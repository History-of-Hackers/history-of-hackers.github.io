Ran Canetti

Ran Canetti is included for documented contributions to the technical and formal foundations of computer security and cryptography.

Ran Canetti is a Boston University computer-science professor and director of RISCS. His work examines how to design and analyze cryptographic protocols that remain secure when used alongside other protocols.

Security relevance

Canetti’s universally composable framework gave protocol designers a rigorous language for composition: a protocol should be assessed not merely in isolation, but in the environment in which it will actually run.

Timeline

  • 1995Completes a Ph.D. at the Weizmann Institute.
  • 1996Joins IBM T. J. Watson Research Center as a research staff member.
  • 2001Publishes the universally composable security framework.
  • 2011Becomes a professor at Boston University and RISCS research director.
