Marc Rogers

Rogers links hands-on technical research with conference stewardship. His public work on connected devices and vehicles helped communicate that digital security has physical-world consequences.

Overview

Marc Rogers is a British information-security researcher whose career combines technical testing, security leadership, and hacker-community organizing. He has been associated with DEF CON for many years and has served as its Head of Security.

Rogers has publicly discussed research on connected technologies, including Tesla systems, Google Glass, and biometric platforms. Work of this kind helps illustrate that vulnerabilities in consumer and vehicle systems can have consequences beyond a conventional desktop computer.

Alongside research, he has held security roles in technology companies and spoken at conferences. His record reflects how community events can connect independent research with wider public discussion of risk.

Timeline

  • 1990s-2000sMoves into professional information-security and ethical-hacking work.
