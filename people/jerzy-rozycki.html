Jerzy Różycki

Różycki’s work was part of the collaborative Polish breakthrough against Enigma. Recording the team, rather than only its most famous member, gives a more accurate account of how cryptanalytic advances happen.

Jerzy Różycki was a Polish mathematician and cryptanalyst assigned to the Cipher Bureau’s German section. Alongside Marian Rejewski and Henryk Zygalski, he contributed to early successful work against Enigma.

Collaborative cryptanalysis

Różycki developed the “clock” method, which used characteristics of Enigma traffic to help determine rotor settings. The Polish team’s results were shared with Allied partners before the Second World War.

In memoriam

Różycki died in 1942 when the passenger ship Lamoricière sank in the Mediterranean. His contribution belongs to the collective history of the Polish Cipher Bureau.

Timeline
