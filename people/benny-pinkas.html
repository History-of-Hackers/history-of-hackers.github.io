Benny Pinkas

Secure computation makes it possible for parties to derive results together without simply pooling all underlying data. Pinkas's work helped turn important multi-party-computation ideas into systems and practical protocols.

Benny Pinkas is a professor at Bar-Ilan University whose research includes applied cryptography, computer security, blockchain security, and privacy.

Secure computation

His research page describes contributions to secure multi-party computation, including privacy-preserving auctions, the Fairplay system, secure AES computation, and private set intersection.

Security research

Pinkas has also published on authentication, random-number-generator security, cloud-storage deduplication, and network-security questions. The account emphasizes research grounded in explicit security assumptions and proofs.

Academic record

Bar-Ilan records his Ph.D. at the Weizmann Institute of Science and lists cryptography, computer security, and private communications as research fields.

Timeline

  • 2000Completes a Ph.D. at the Weizmann Institute of Science.
  • 2004Co-authors the Fairplay secure-computation system.
