Kenneth Geers

Geers has helped translate technical-security developments into policy and strategic context. His work on cyber conflict, including Ukraine, made questions of attribution, resilience, and international security accessible to both technical and policy audiences.

Overview

Kenneth Geers is a cybersecurity researcher with a background in international relations, intelligence analysis, and cyber policy. Biographies from NATO-related and academic forums record roles across the U.S. government, NCIS, NATO, private-sector threat research, and university teaching.

His work considers cyber operations in their political and strategic setting, rather than treating incidents as purely technical events. He was involved in the early development of NATO's Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence after the 2007 attacks on Estonia.

Geers has written and edited work on strategic cybersecurity and cyber conflict, including a volume on Ukraine and cyber war. This record recognizes policy research that helps contextualize technical threats and defensive choices.
