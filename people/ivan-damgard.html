Ivan Bjerre Damgård

Damgård's work helped shape core cryptographic constructions and secure multi-party computation, making him a key academic contributor to modern protocol theory.

Ivan Bjerre Damgård is a professor at Aarhus University and heads its cryptography group. His research includes theoretical and applied cryptography and secure computation.

From hashing to protocols

The Merkle–Damgård construction is a standard way to extend a fixed-input-length compression function into a hash function. Damgård has also made major contributions to protocols for computing jointly on protected data.

Security by construction

His work illustrates how formal cryptographic definitions and proofs can guide the design of systems that must preserve secrecy or correctness even when participants do not fully trust one another.
