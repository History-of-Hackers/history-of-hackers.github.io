Paul Asadoorian

Asadoorian helped build a durable public forum for security practitioners through Security Weekly. His combination of technical practice, books, conference presentations, and long-form interviews has connected research to a broad professional audience.

Overview

Paul Asadoorian is a security practitioner and communicator who founded Security Weekly, a podcast network covering information security and hacking. He has held technical roles in universities, consulting, and security-product companies.

His technical interests include penetration testing, vulnerability management, embedded devices, firmware, and supply-chain security. In 2007 he co-authored WRT54G Ultimate Hacking, a book focused on embedded-device and wireless experimentation.

Security Weekly gave practitioners a recurring forum for news, technical discussion, and interviews. Its community value is rooted in sustained, accessible conversation rather than a single vulnerability or product.
