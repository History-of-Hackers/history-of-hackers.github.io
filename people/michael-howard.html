Michael Howard

Howard's work helped popularize the idea that software security needs a disciplined lifecycle, with design review, testing, and accountability built into product development.

Secure development

Michael Howard is a software-security engineer and author closely associated with Microsoft's secure-development work. His career has focused on reducing recurring classes of software flaws by making security requirements part of the engineering process.

Writing Secure Code

With David LeBlanc, Howard co-authored Writing Secure Code, a widely used practitioner reference. The book explains common implementation risks and the design and review habits that can prevent them.

Lifecycle thinking

Howard's work is associated with the Security Development Lifecycle approach: security activities planned across requirements, design, implementation, test, release, and response. The lasting contribution is organizational as well as technical—security becomes a repeatable engineering responsibility.
