Sarah Meiklejohn

Meiklejohn’s research challenges simplistic claims of cryptocurrency anonymity by analyzing public ledgers as adversarial evidence.

Sarah Meiklejohn is a cryptography and security researcher affiliated with University College London’s Information Security Group and the Initiative for Cryptocurrencies and Contracts. Her research spans cryptocurrencies, cryptography, and the security implications of public-ledger systems.

Following evidence in public ledgers

Her work on Bitcoin and related ecosystems combines empirical measurement with security analysis. It shows that public transaction records can expose relationships and support investigative inferences even where systems are described as anonymous.

Security relevance

By applying rigorous analysis to cryptocurrency infrastructure and underground markets, this work connects cryptographic mechanisms to the observable behavior of real systems.
