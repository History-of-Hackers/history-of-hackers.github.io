Haya Shulman

Shulman’s research addresses security properties of core Internet infrastructure. It helps show why protocol assumptions, resolver behavior and deployment choices can create systemic risk.

Haya Shulman is a computer-security researcher whose work examines DNS, routing and the security of Internet infrastructure. Her research has addressed vulnerabilities and operational assumptions in systems that users often take for granted.

Infrastructure security

DNS and routing security are ecosystem problems: improvements depend on software, operators, standards and deployment. Shulman’s research uses technical analysis and measurement to make those dependencies visible and to motivate mitigations.

Research context

Her academic and institutional profiles document work across network security and Internet measurement, including leadership roles in security research groups.
