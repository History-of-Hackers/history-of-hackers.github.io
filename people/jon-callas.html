Jon Callas

Callas has worked at the intersection of practical cryptographic tools, usability, and civil liberties—questions central to how people can protect private communications in real systems.

Practical cryptography

Jon Callas is a cryptographer and privacy engineer whose work has included PGP, OpenPGP, and secure communications products. His career has centered on turning cryptographic ideas into tools that people and organizations can actually use.

Open standards and privacy

Callas has contributed to the OpenPGP ecosystem and has held technology roles in industry and at the Electronic Frontier Foundation. His work highlights the practical challenges of key management, interoperability, and user experience in privacy-preserving systems.

Public-interest technology

At EFF, Callas's technical leadership connected security engineering with civil-liberties advocacy. The role reflects an enduring hacker-culture concern: strong tools matter most when users retain meaningful control over their communications and data.
